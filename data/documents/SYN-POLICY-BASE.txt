SYNTHETIC DEMO — Student Academic Services Policy

Fictional policy created only to demonstrate the hackathon system. This is NOT an official DTU policy.

Issuer: HCLTech hackathon demo (fictional)

2.1 Attendance
A student must have at least 75 percent attendance in a registered course to appear in an end-semester examination.

2.2 Attendance condonation
A student with attendance from 65 percent up to the minimum may request condonation for no more than two courses.

2.3 Cross-reference
The condonation request is subject to clause 2.1 and approval by the fictional Academic Services Office.

3.1 Pass marks
A pass requires at least 40 marks out of 100 total marks.

3.2 External marks
A pass also requires at least 24 marks out of 60 external marks.

3.3 Results
ABSENT and DETAINED are not passing results.

4.1 Supplementary eligibility
Supplementary assessment is available for FAIL or ABSENT in no more than two courses, but not for DETAINED.

4.2 Procedure
The student submits the fictional form to the Academic Services Office within seven calendar days of result publication.

5.1 Placement CGPA
For this fictional demo only, placement eligibility requires CGPA of at least 7.0.

5.2 Placement backlogs
For this fictional demo only, active backlogs must be no more than 2.

5.3 Placement result condition
A student with a DETAINED result is not eligible in this fictional demo.

6. Exception
A documented accessibility or medical exception may be reviewed by the fictional committee; it does not change the calculated result automatically.
